## Input devices

The Magic Remote's keys reach a full-screen SDL app as these scancodes: LEFT 80,
UP 82, RIGHT 79, DOWN 81 (SDL's standard codes), OK 40 (Return) and BACK 482.
Scancodes 484 and 485 are the pointer showing and hiding, sent as keys:
SDL-webOS names them `SDL_SCANCODE_WEBOS_CURSOR_SHOW` and
`SDL_SCANCODE_WEBOS_CURSOR_HIDE`, and the shell's 2026-10-22 logs agree (484
just before the pointer moves, 485 about 5 s after it stops). While the
pointer shows, OK arrives as a left mouse click, not as scancode 40 (c500289 and
df2a4d0 runs, 2026-10-22). HOME never reaches the app: the compositor's key
filters handle it and open LG's Home.

**The remote's raw events** (a read-only `cat` of the nodes during a run,
2026-10-22; the numbering may change across boots). `event1` carries only
POWER (116). `event3` is the Magic Remote: the arrows (103, 108, 105, 106),
OK (28), a click (BTN_LEFT, 272), the pointer's position as absolute X and
Y, and LG's own codes above Linux's KEY_MAX (767): 1198 and 1199 for the
pointer showing and hiding (SDL's 484 and 485), and 773, held 150–200 ms
just before each trip through LG's launcher, probably Home. A 139
(KEY_MENU) comes with the pointer appearing, pressed and released within
1 ms, so it is generated, not a button. `event4` stayed empty. Each record
is 16 bytes (`tv_sec u32, tv_usec u32, type u16, code u16, value s32`),
stamped on CLOCK_REALTIME. All 75 presses that came while Lumen's shell had
the focus reached it, 3–37 ms after the kernel's stamp.

**LG's input devices are virtual** (`/devices/virtual/input`), created by its
services from user space: `CHECK INPUT`, `LGE RCU`, `Bluetooth-audio-source`,
two `LGE M-RCU - Builtin` (the Magic Remote, with a pointer), `LGE Simple
Premium` and `IoT keypad`. `/dev/input/event*` are `root:composit` 0660.

**A wired Xbox Series controller binds but sends no input.** Plugged in by
USB, it enumerated as `045e:0b12` at full speed on an OHCI port. The built-in
`xpad` driver bound its first interface as "Generic X-Box pad" on `event7`,
declaring buttons, both sticks, the triggers, the D-pad and rumble; its other
two interfaces stay unbound. A 15-second read of `event7` while every control
was used returned no bytes (2026-10-21). No process held the node open, so
nothing had grabbed it, and the kernel logged no `xpad` error. The pad's Xbox
button kept blinking the whole time; it goes solid once a host completes the
pad's start-up handshake. So this kernel's `xpad` (Linux 4.4), older than the
Series pad (2020), most likely never sends the start-up packets the pad needs.

**Over Bluetooth, the same pad pairs but never connects as input**
(2026-10-21, through `com.webos.service.bluetooth2`):
* **Pairing works:** discovery found it as "Xbox Wireless Controller", a BLE
  device, and `adapter/pair` finished with `endPairing` and `paired: true`.
* **HID never connects:** `hid/connect` was accepted but never reported a
  connection, `connectedProfiles` stayed empty, and no input device appeared.
* **The GATT link drops:** with the pad freshly woken, `gatt/connect` returned
  success, but the link was gone before `gatt/getServices` (error 136, "Device
  is not connected to profile"). The pad's light blinked and went off, never
  solid.

The service offers `/hid` (`connect`, `disconnect`, `getStatus`),
`/hid/internal`, `/gatt`, `/le` and the usual audio and file profiles. There is no
`/dev/input/js*`, because `joydev` isn't built, so readers use evdev, as SDL
does.

**Drivers built into the kernel** (`/proc/config.gz`): `xpad` with force
feedback, `usbhid`, `hid-generic`, `hid-sony`, `hid-logitech`, `dragonrise`,
`pantherlord`, `acrux` and `zeroplus`, plus `uinput`, `uhid`, `hidraw` and
`evdev`. There is no `hid-microsoft`. `/dev/uinput` and `/dev/uhid` exist and
are root-only (0600).

## Lumen notes

Measurements and surprises that matter to Lumen, beyond the profile above.

**Graphics and IPC libraries** in `/usr/lib`: SDL2 2.0.5
(`libSDL2-2.0.so.0.4.1`), SDL2_image, SDL2_mixer, SDL2_net and SDL2_ttf,
`libGLESv2`, `libEGL`, `libEGLfk`, `libwayland-client`, `libluna-service2`
3.21.2 (and its C++ binding), `libplayerAPIs`, `libAcbAPI`. Qt 5 QML modules
include QtQuick, QtMultimedia and the compositor's `KeyFilters`.

**LG's SDL exports five webOS calls** beyond upstream's:
`SDL_webOSCursorVisibility`, `SDL_webOSGetFlashInfo`,
`SDL_webOSGetPanelResolution`, `SDL_webOSGetRamInfo` and
`SDL_webOSGetRefreshRate` (`grep -a` of `libSDL2-2.0.so.0`, 2026-10-22).
Lumen's shell hides the pointer with the first when an arrow is pressed.

**Hardware float works.** The A53's VFPv4 and NEON run code built for the
soft-float ABI target with `-Ctarget-feature=-soft-float,+vfp4,+neon`
(floats still in core registers). Lumen's shell built that way started in
524 and 688 ms instead of 1,312–1,594 ms, its font set-up 5.5 times faster
(2026-10-23).

**hello-gles** loaded the system SDL and drew 1920×1080 with vsync at a
steady 60.0 fps: 16.67 ms average, 17–19 ms worst frame per second, at about
3,000 quads in one batched draw.

**SDL key events are laid out differently from desktop SDL.** In
`SDL_KeyboardEvent`, the TV's SDL puts `state` at byte 16, `repeat` at 17 and
the keysym's scancode at 20 (desktop SDL 2: 12, 13 and 16), with an unknown
4-byte field at 12. Because of this, BACK did not close hello-gles.

**A backgrounded app keeps drawing.** After HOME opened LG's Home, hello-gles
went on rendering at 60 fps until it was closed.

**The compositor's key filters** (`com.webos.surfacemanager.keyFilters`) are 11
handlers from `systemUi.js` and `appLaunch.js` in
`/usr/lib/qt5/qml/KeyFilters`; `handleSystemKeys` is fifth.

**Screensaver:** `com.webos.app.screensaver`, type `qml`, main file
`/usr/palm/applications/com.webos.app.screensaver/qml/main.qml`. LG starts
it 110–116 s after the last input, over a full-screen app too: a client
subscribed to `tvpower/power/registerScreenSaverRequest` is asked first,
then the power state turns `Screen Saver` and the app in front loses the
focus within 0.8 s (six times, 2026-10-22 and 23). The press that wakes the
TV goes to LG, not to the app. Left alone, this set stays in the
screensaver: 16.6 h in a row on 2026-10-22, which the owner says is how it
always behaves.

**Homebrew Channel:**
- Installed; `/var/lib/webosbrew/init.d` is empty.
- Set in `/var/luna/preferences`: `devmode_enabled`, `webosbrew_block_updates`,
  `webosbrew_sshd_enabled`, `webosbrew_telnet_disabled`.
- Glasshouse is not installed.

**Gotchas met while connecting:**
- Turning SSH on in Homebrew Channel opened port 22 only after a cold boot
  (unplugging the set). With Quick Start+, the remote's power button is
  standby, not a reboot.
- Over SSH, `luna-send -f` prints nothing unless its stdin is `/dev/null`.

## Home

Read on 2026-10-31 in three read-only passes over SSH (raw output in
`tools/out/home-look-20261031-*.raw.txt`, kept out of the repository because
it holds LG's scripts), with Netflix in front, 3 h 58 min after a boot.

* **LG Home is a QML app behind a 39 KB native loader.**
  `/usr/palm/applications/com.webos.app.home/appinfo.json`: type `native`,
  main `/usr/bin/com.webos.app.home`, which loads `qml/main.qml` from the
  app's own folder. Its `defaultWindowType` is `home` (the compositor's
  overlay view, drawn over the running app), it is `transparent`,
  `visible: false`, `appCategory: ["home"]`,
  `nativeLifeCycleInterfaceVersion: 2`. `com.webos.app.homeconnect` ("Home
  Dashboard") is a web app, and LG's full-screen alternative,
  `com.webos.app.fullhome`, is installed as well.
* **The Home key is answered by the compositor's `systemUi.js`.**
  `handleSystemKeys` sends Home (Qt's `Key_Super_L`), Menu and Recent to
  `handleHomeKeys`. On the release of a short press it calls
  `applicationManager.launch(<default home>, {"launchType":"homeKey"})`. A
  press held 1,000 ms takes the long-press branch: for `com.webos.app.home`
  a relaunch with `activateType: recent`, for `com.webos.app.fullhome` the
  most recent app, and for any other id nothing at all. `appLaunch.js`'s
  `handleTvhotkeyAppLaunch` handles factory hotkeys only, not Home. Home is
  ignored during the first-use wizard and when `settingsService.disableHome`
  is set (`com.webos.surfacemanager.disableHome` is `false` here).
* **The default home is an LG setting.** The compositor's
  `SystemSettings.qml` reads `defaultApps.home` from
  `com.webos.settingsservice` (`getSystemSettings`, category `general`, key
  `defaultApps`); on this set it is `{"home": "com.webos.app.home"}`. LG's
  own `/etc/init.d/home_launcher.sh` (job `home_launcher`, at
  `core-boot-done`) reads the same key, writes it with `setSystemSettings`
  on one model, and force-closes whichever of `com.webos.app.home` and
  `com.webos.app.fullhome` is not selected. LG switches homes by changing a
  setting, not by replacing files.
* **How native apps run.** SAM is `/usr/sbin/sam` (job `sam`, respawns,
  `start on ls-hubd_public-ready and lsm-ready`, stopped for firmware
  updates). Apps run through `/usr/bin/jailer` (root only, mode 0700) with
  per-type configs in `/etc/jail_*.conf`: developer-mode native apps use
  `jail_native_devmode.conf`, LG's built-in native apps
  `jail_native_builtin.conf`, and the two differ mostly in developer-mode
  SSH keys and `/var/run`. Each jailed app has its own uid in group 5000 and
  a root under `/var/palm/jail/<id>`: Lumen's shell is uid 5727, plx-native
  5137, Netflix 5644, YouTube 6490, Amazon 5076. The compositor
  (`surface-manager-starfish`, job `surface-manager`) and `bootd` run as
  root. LG Home was not running during the look; `sam.conf` treats
  `@system_native_app` processes specially on a respawn, and SAM's
  `keepAliveApps` config lists both LG homes.
* **Boot.** `/etc/palm/bootd.json`: the first app after a normal boot is
  `{LAST_INPUT_APP}` (here `com.webos.app.hdmi3`), with the Netflix and
  Amazon buttons overriding it; Home is not the first app. The upstart task
  `com.webos.app.home` preloads LG Home (`preload: full`) only on snapshot
  boots. `homeAutoLaunch` is `off`, `homeAutoLaunchMode` `full`,
  `quickStartMode` `off`.
* **Restarting SAM** is an ordinary `initctl restart sam`; `surface-manager`
  is its own job too. Neither is needed for the route below.

**What it means.** The lowest-risk takeover is LG's own switch: set
`defaultApps.home` to Lumen's id through `settingsservice`, as LG does for
its full-screen home. A short Home press then launches Lumen; a long press
does nothing until Lumen gives it a meaning; LG Home stays installed and is
reached from Lumen (a tile, or `applicationManager/launch`) or by setting the
key back. Untried: whether `settingsservice` accepts an id outside LG's two,
and how the compositor's `homeView.launchDefaultApp(..., {"category":"home"})`
path, used when the last app closes, treats a fullscreen app. That is the
first trial, by hand, after a backup.

**Trial, 2026-10-31 05:46 UTC** (after a backup, Netflix in front, 3 h 58 min
after a boot, load average 11). `setSystemSettings` accepted Lumen's id
(`returnValue: true`, read back as `{"home": "io.github.sunnyside007.lumen.shell"}`).
A short Home press launched Lumen at once: the compositor's call arrived as
launch parameters `{"@system_native_app": true, "launchType": "homeKey",
"nid": "<Lumen's id>"}`, Lumen became the foreground app, and its cold start
took 1,173 ms to the first frame (sdl 264, gl 622, renderer 797, assets
1055, apps 1130; slower than the 524 to 688 ms measured on an idle set, with
Netflix still running behind it). 30 tiles, all with icons. Afterwards SAM
listed `com.webos.app.home` as running too. Route D works on this firmware;
a long Home press does nothing with Lumen as the default, as the handler
reads.
The owner kept the setting on the same day: since 2026-10-31 05:50 UTC Lumen
is this set's Home. The undo is the same call with `com.webos.app.home`.

Seen again by the R0 survey later the same day (`remote-20261031-060938.txt`):
LG Home was running, preloaded at boot (`snapshot: true`, `preload: full`,
`@system_native_app`), as **root with every capability, root `/` and init's
mount namespace**: unjailed. Lumen, launched by the same Home key, stays a
jailed developer-mode app (uid 5727). Route D keeps the home screen in the

**The boot hook that closes it**, 2026-10-31 (`takeover/lumen-home`; raw
output in `tools/out/takeover/`). After a full `reboot` LG Home was not

## Resolver

Measured on 2026-10-31 while trying Lumen Guard by hand (privacy.md, G1).

* **connman owns the resolver.** `/etc/resolv.conf` is a symlink to
  `/var/lib/misc/resolv.conf`, which connman writes with `nameserver
  127.0.0.1`: its own proxy, which forwards to the router the DHCP lease
  names (no `Nameservers=` line in `/var/lib/connman/*/settings`). glibc is
  2.24, which reads `resolv.conf` when a process first resolves, not on
  change; the app jails `copy /etc/resolv.conf` at launch.
* **connman's forwarding sockets are bound to `wlan0`,** and it keeps one
  per upstream. A nat `OUTPUT` rule that redirects its forwards to a local
  address therefore cannot work in either direction: aimed at `127.0.0.2`
  the rewritten packet never reaches the local stack (the rule matched, the
  kernel's `Udp NoPorts` counter stayed flat); aimed at the TV's own
  `wlan0` address the queries do arrive, and a forwarder listening there
