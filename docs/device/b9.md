## Input devices

The Magic Remote's keys reach a full-screen SDL app as these scancodes: LEFT 80,
UP 82, RIGHT 79, DOWN 81 (SDL's standard codes), OK 40 (Return) and BACK 482.
Scancodes 484 and 485 arrive around pointer movement, probably the pointer
showing and hiding (not confirmed). HOME never reaches the app: the
compositor's key filters handle it and open LG's Home.

## Lumen notes

Measurements and surprises that matter to Lumen, beyond the profile above.

**Graphics and IPC libraries** in `/usr/lib`: SDL2 2.0.5
(`libSDL2-2.0.so.0.4.1`), SDL2_image, SDL2_mixer, SDL2_net and SDL2_ttf,
`libGLESv2`, `libEGL`, `libEGLfk`, `libwayland-client`, `libluna-service2`
3.21.2 (and its C++ binding), `libplayerAPIs`, `libAcbAPI`. Qt 5 QML modules
include QtQuick, QtMultimedia and the compositor's `KeyFilters`.

**hello-gles** loaded the system SDL and drew 1920×1080 with vsync at a
steady 60.0 fps: 16.67 ms average, 17–19 ms worst frame per second, at about
3,000 quads in one batched draw.

**SDL key events are laid out differently from desktop SDL.** In
`SDL_KeyboardEvent`, the TV's SDL puts `state` at byte 16, `repeat` at 17 and
the keysym's scancode at 20 (desktop SDL 2: 12, 13 and 16), with an unknown
4-byte field at 12. Because of this, BACK did not close hello-gles.

**A backgrounded app keeps drawing.** After HOME opened LG's Home, hello-gles
went on rendering at 60 fps until it was closed.

**The compositor's key filters** (`com.webos.surfacemanager.keyFilters`) are 11
handlers from `systemUi.js` and `appLaunch.js` in
`/usr/lib/qt5/qml/KeyFilters`; `handleSystemKeys` is fifth.

**Screensaver:** `com.webos.app.screensaver`, type `qml`, main file
`/usr/palm/applications/com.webos.app.screensaver/qml/main.qml`.

**Homebrew Channel:**
- Installed; `/var/lib/webosbrew/init.d` is empty.
- Set in `/var/luna/preferences`: `devmode_enabled`, `webosbrew_block_updates`,
  `webosbrew_sshd_enabled`, `webosbrew_telnet_disabled`.
- Glasshouse is not installed.

**Gotchas met while connecting:**
- Turning SSH on in Homebrew Channel opened port 22 only after a cold boot
  (unplugging the set). With Quick Start+, the remote's power button is
  standby, not a reboot.
- Over SSH, `luna-send -f` prints nothing unless its stdin is `/dev/null`.
